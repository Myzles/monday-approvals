Get sign-off on any monday.com item

Ask one person or ten. In order, all at once, or first answer wins. Approvers answer from one inbox, and every decision is posted to the item under the name of the person who made it.

Add to monday.com

Three ways to ask

One after another, everyone must approve, or the first approval is enough. Any rejection stops the request.

One inbox for approvers

"Waiting on me" lists every open request across boards, sorted by due date. Approve or reject without opening the item.

Works with automations

Pick a status column and it changes to Waiting for approval, Approved or Rejected. Build any monday automation on it.

Reasons on rejections

Rejecting asks for a reason by default, so the requester knows what to fix.

Hand-offs and due dates

Someone away? Hand the request to a colleague. Overdue requests are flagged.

Your data stays in monday

Requests and decisions are stored in monday's own app storage, and every action runs with the signed-in person's permissions. Only Sidekick (AI) calls pass through a small Cloudflare Worker that stores nothing.

Everything waiting on you, in one place

Add the Approvals inbox view to any board. Approvers see what needs them across the whole account, with the reason, the amount and the deadline right there.

Approved? Your automations take it from there

Map results to a status column once per board. When it flips to Approved, move the item, notify a channel, or start the next task with the automations you already use.
