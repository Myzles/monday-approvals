Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026. This policy covers the "Approvals" app for monday.com, published by Myzles ("we").

What the app stores

Approval requests (title, details, due date, approvers, decisions, comments and timestamps), small indexes that power the approval inbox, board settings, and a monthly count of requests used. All of it is kept in monday.com's own app storage for your account.

Where your data goes

Requests and decisions are stored in monday.com's own app storage. The board and item views run inside monday.com and talk only to monday.com. If you use the Sidekick (AI) skill, Sidekick's calls go through a small Cloudflare Worker that checks the request came from monday, passes data to and from monday.com, and does not store or log item content. The app does not send your data to any other third party, does not use cookies or tracking, does not sell data, and does not use your data to train any model.

Permissions

The app reads users and boards so you can pick approvers and a status column, and writes item updates, status column values and notifications when a request or decision is made. Every action runs with the permissions of the person signed in.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app removes access to its storage. To have stored data deleted sooner, email the address below from an account admin's address and include your monday account name.
